Privacy policy

This policy explains how Stackspire, an Android game developed by Saadhat (package com.flipcircuit.game), handles information. Contact: saadhat10@gmail.com.

Progress saved on your device

Stackspire stores game progress, scores, coins, unlocks, goals, saved towers and settings locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives this local inventory or synchronizes it between devices. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes its local data. These actions can permanently remove your progress.

Google Play Games

The game integrates Google Play Games Services for player authentication and leaderboards. Google may provide a player identifier, gamer profile and authentication status. Eligible scores are submitted to Google Play Games so you can compare results; your gamer profile and submitted scores may be visible to other players according to your Google settings. Stackspire does not receive your Google password. Core gameplay does not require a successful Play Games connection.

You can manage your Play Games profile and game data through Google's Play Games settings. Removing Stackspire's local storage does not necessarily delete scores or information stored by Google.

Advertising and consent

Stackspire uses Google's AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to load rewarded and interstitial advertisements, and Google's User Messaging Platform to handle applicable consent and privacy choices. These services may process IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from them), device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, ad views and clicks, diagnostics and device information. Google and its advertising partners may use this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and personalize advertising where permitted.

Where required, a consent message appears before advertising is requested. The game's Settings includes Ad choices to reopen available privacy options. You can also manage or reset your advertising identifier through Android settings. Ad availability and privacy options vary by region.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Sharing and support

If you choose to share a result, Android lets you select the receiving app. That app handles the information under its own policy. The game does not automatically post results to social networks. If you email support, we receive the address and information you send, use them to respond and resolve the request, and retain correspondence only as needed for support and legitimate recordkeeping.

Permissions, storage and security

The game uses internet access for Google services and ads, and vibration for optional haptic feedback. It does not request access to your microphone, contacts or precise location. Saved game records remain in app storage unless you choose to export or share them. Google handles information sent to its services under its own retention and security policies.

Children

Stackspire is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through support, contact us so we can address it.

Your requests

For questions, access or deletion requests relating to information you sent directly to us, contact saadhat10@gmail.com. For data held by Google, use your Google Account and Play Games data controls or contact Google. Stackspire does not create a separate developer account for you.

Policy changes

We may update this page as game features or service integrations change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
