Delete Stackspire data

Stackspire is an Android game by Saadhat. It does not create a separate developer account.

Game data on your device

In Android Settings, open Apps, select Stackspire, then Storage and choose Clear storage or Clear data. Alternatively, uninstall the app. This permanently removes locally stored progress, scores, coins, unlocks, saved towers and settings. Menu names vary by device.

Google Play Games data

Clearing local storage does not necessarily remove your Play Games profile or leaderboard data. Use Google's instructions for deleting Play Games data to manage data stored by Google. Google controls its own retention and deletion processes.

Request deletion of support correspondence

Email saadhat10@gmail.com from the address you used to contact support. Ask to delete your Stackspire support correspondence and identify the relevant request; do not send passwords. We use correspondence for support and retain it only as needed for support and legitimate recordkeeping; no fixed retention period has been specified. Google-held data is handled separately through Google's controls.
